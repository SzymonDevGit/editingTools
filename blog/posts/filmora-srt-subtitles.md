Wondershare Filmora has AI speech-to-text, but it runs on a plan or credits. Filmora also imports ordinary SRT files, so you can make the subtitles for free with Whisper and only use Filmora for styling and export.

## 1. Make the SRT

Open [AutoSubtitler](/), drop in your video and press **Make subtitles**. Download the .srt when it's done. Your video stays on your computer.

## 2. Import it

Import the .srt into the media library like any other file, either with **File › Import Media** or by dragging it in. Then drag it onto the timeline above your video. Filmora turns each line into a subtitle clip.

## 3. Edit and style

Double-click a subtitle to open the subtitle editor. There you can fix text, adjust timing, and change the font, colour and background. Most versions can apply a style to all subtitles at once.

## 4. Export

Exported videos have the subtitles burned in. Keep the .srt from step 1 if you also want separate captions for YouTube. See [burned-in vs separate subtitles](/blog/burned-in-vs-separate-subtitles/).

## Tips

- The menu names in Filmora change between versions. If drag-and-drop doesn't work, look for **Import Subtitle** in the Titles or Subtitles section.
- Short one-line chunks, the AutoSubtitler default, suit vertical video. Switch to two-line sentences for interviews.
