VSDC Free Video Editor is a free editor for Windows with a lot of features for its price. One of them is a **subtitles object** that can load an SRT file. Make the SRT for free with Whisper and VSDC handles the rest.

## 1. Make the SRT

Open [AutoSubtitler](/), drop in your video and press **Make subtitles**. Download the .srt. The video stays on your computer.

## 2. Add a subtitles object

1. Open your project in VSDC with the video on the timeline.
2. Use the **Add object** menu (or the toolbar on the left) and choose **Subtitles**.
3. Place the object at the start of your video, then load your .srt file into it. Depending on your version, there's a button for this in the object's properties or in the subtitle editor.

## 3. Edit and style

Double-click the subtitles object to open its editor, where you can fix lines and timings. The **Properties** window sets the font, size, colour, outline and position.

## 4. Export

On the **Export project** tab, pick a format and export. The subtitles are drawn into the picture. Keep the .srt from step 1 if you also want separate captions for YouTube.

## Tips

- Make sure the subtitles object starts at the same time as the video it was made from, or every line will be shifted.
- VSDC's menus differ a little between versions. If you can't find the subtitle option, search VSDC's help for "subtitles".
